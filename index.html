My blood drips into the neon gutters. I never expected to die on this planet.

The dark red trickles down my arm, pooling on the grip of my blaster.

I was hired to watch: Shadow her, report to her father, then vanish like a ghost—

The whole mission a secret.

But in the rain-soaked alleys beneath the old maglev tracks, the Censors appeared. One flash of a pulse rifle, one split-second choice, and now two bodies bleed out in the gutter behind us.

Now I’m running from the authorities with Taliah Taiberos—the senator’s daughter.

She was never supposed to know I existed. A girl with wildfire hair and rebellion in her eyes. I can’t deny the attraction I feel. Her hand is in mine, and every step we take together could be our last.

Taliah wants to destroy her father’s empire.

I just want to survive.

Stop the bleeding. Or I’m going to be the next corpse face-down in the gutter.

The city is a labyrinth, and every shadow hides a hunter—their mirror visors dotted with rain. With every heartbeat, I feel the net tightening. I’m no longer a bystander. I’m implicated.

I’ve touched the secret. Been tainted by it.

And on a planet where censorship rules…

Secrets get buried.

About the Author

A.J. Corynne writes character-driven adventures with high-stakes plots, wondrous settings, and spicy romantic chemistry.

And since you made it past that first sentence, I’ll let you in on a little secret. A.J. Corynne isn’t my real name. And we’re actually two people, a secret dual-author collab. Why secret? Well—and this is where it gets real—one of us helped rescue a child from abuse and suffered ongoing repercussions for it. The pseudonym helps protect the innocent from further harm.

But you’ll find that our personalities shine right through the Zorro mask, represented in clues by a few of our favorite things:

  1. A minimalist leather wallet about half a centimeter thick
  2. An orange-crayon tiger drawing from a first-grader
  3. A clacky Keychron keyboard with a red escape key
  4. A wooden cup of the carpenter that sits on a shelf right back there
  5. A packet of kale seedlings waiting for spring
  6. A worn out copy of Pride and Prejudice that might in fact be stolen

I’m glad you’re here. We have some exciting things ahead.
